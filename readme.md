## Chosen Datasets
- Catholic Churches in Arizona:  shows the distribution of religious institutions in Arizona that serve local communities by providing worship services, education and other community resources. Mapping these locations helps reveal patterns in religious infrastructure and how churches are distributed across urban and rural areas.
- High Schools in Arizona: shows the locations of high schools across the state. It represents educational facilities that serve Arizona's student population. Analyzing the spatial distribution of high schools can help identify areas with strong access to education, population centers and regional differences in educational resources.

These two datasets are related because both represent community-serving institutions that are often located where people live.


## JSON
Json files contains coordinates of schools and churches and provide code for interective map features (pop-ups, layer controls, map/satellite view)

## Markers
Blue markers indicate Catholic Churches and Red markers indicate Schools.

Pop-ups contain the name, latitude and longtitude.

## Analyses 
Comparing spatial distribution of High Schools and Catholic Churches in Arizona shows heavy clustering within major urban centers, specifically Maricopa County (Phoenix) and Pima County (Tucson). The close proximity of schools and churches in these urban areas indicate great community service infrastructure and high demographic demand. 

On the other hand, rural and tribal regions show lower distributions, indicating lower population density and greater geographic isolation.

In conclusion, both schools and churches are often near each other because both serve local communities.
